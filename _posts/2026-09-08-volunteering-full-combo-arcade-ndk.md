Over Labor Day weekend, I had the chance to help out [Full Combo Arcade](https://fullcomboarcade.com/) at **Nan Desu Kan (NDK) 2026**, Colorado's premier anime convention held at the Gaylord Rockies Resort in Aurora, CO.

Full Combo Arcade provides authentic Japanese arcade experiences for conventions and events across the region, bringing dedicated rhythm game cabinets directly to attendees. As an avid rhythm gamer, it was great to spend time at the con, catch up with friends, and help support the event.

![Full Combo Arcade Ballroom Setup]({{ '/assets/img/ndk/20260903_225230.jpg' | relative_url }})
*The convention ballroom transformed with rows of Japanese rhythm game cabinets powered up and ready for attendees.*

### The Con and the Arcade Hall

Throughout the weekend, the arcade ballroom was packed with energy as convention-goers enjoyed an incredible lineup of machines—including *Chunithm*, *Ongeki*, *maimai DX*, *SOUND VOLTEX*, *Taiko no Tatsujin*, *Groove Coaster*, *Jubeat*, *Wacca*, *Project DIVA Future Tone*, *Museca*, *Pump It Up Phoenix*, and *DanceDanceRevolution*. It was awesome seeing both veteran rhythm game players and newcomers crowding around cabs to watch high-difficulty clears and try out games rarely found outside Japan.

![Crowd Playing Rhythm Games at NDK]({{ '/assets/img/ndk/20260904_152910.jpg' | relative_url }})
*Convention attendees enjoying games like Project DIVA Future Tone, Ongeki Summer, Chunithm, and maimai.*

### Transportation and Cleanup

My volunteer support was primarily focused on driving and transporting people, as well as helping with teardown and cleanup once the convention wrapped up:

- **Transportation:** Helping shuttle people to and from the venue across the busy convention weekend.
- **Teardown and Cleanup:** Packing down an entire convention arcade hall after several days of non-stop play takes a serious team effort. When the con came to a close on Sunday, I helped with clearing out the room, wrapping up gear, and moving cabinets so the space could be packed up smoothly.

![Friends and fellow volunteers at NDK 2026]({{ '/assets/img/ndk/P1011700_1.jpg' | relative_url }})
*Hanging out in the arcade hall with friends and fellow volunteers at NDK 2026 (wearing my Colorado School of Mines EE hat!).*

### Wrap-Up

It was a great weekend spending time with friends in the local rhythm gaming community, helping out with rides and final cleanup, and seeing everyone enjoy the arcade space. Big shoutout to Full Combo Arcade for another fantastic event!
