Over Labor Day weekend, I had the privilege of volunteering with [Full Combo Arcade](https://fullcomboarcade.com/) at **Nan Desu Kan (NDK) 2026**, Colorado's largest and longest-running anime convention held at the Gaylord Rockies Resort in Aurora, CO.

As a long-time rhythm game enthusiast and electrical/computer engineer, working behind the scenes with Full Combo Arcade was an absolute blast. Full Combo Arcade is dedicated to providing authentic Japanese arcade experiences for anime and gaming conventions across the country, bringing rare imported rhythm games and dedicated hardware directly to fans who might otherwise never get to experience them.

### Behind the Scenes: Logistics, Power, and Calibration

Setting up a full-scale Japanese arcade in a convention hotel ballroom is a serious engineering and logistical challenge that starts well before attendees arrive:

- **Cabinet Load-In & Floor Planning:** Rolling dozens of heavy, delicate CRT and high-refresh LCD cabs into place, organizing rows to optimize attendee flow and noise management. The lineup this year was stellar: *Chunithm*, *Ongeki*, *maimai DX*, *SOUND VOLTEX*, *Taiko no Tatsujin*, *Groove Coaster*, *Jubeat*, *Wacca*, *Project DIVA Future Tone*, *Museca*, *Pump It Up Phoenix*, and *DanceDanceRevolution*.
- **Power & Thermal Management:** Ensuring hundreds of amps of power distribution are cleanly balanced across hotel drops without tripping breakers, while managing cable raceways so thousands of walking attendees never encounter trip hazards.
- **Hardware Calibration & Testing:** Tuning touch sliders on Chunithm, checking laser encoders and microswitches on SDVX, dialing in audio levels and headphone jacks, and verifying local network infrastructure for cabinet synchronization and profile cards.

### The Con Floor: Keeping the Music Playing

Once the doors opened on Friday, the energy in the arcade hall was non-stop through Sunday evening:

- **Welcoming Newcomers & Fostering Community:** Many attendees had seen games like *Chunithm* or *maimai* in viral videos but had never laid hands on one. Introducing new players to the basics, explaining touch sensors and motion air-strings, and helping people set up IC cards was immensely rewarding.
- **On-the-Fly Hardware Maintenance:** With cabinets seeing continuous, aggressive play for 14+ hours a day, keeping machines running smoothly required rapid troubleshooting. From button lubrication and microswitch adjustments to sensor re-calibrations, my engineering background felt right at home keeping downtime to an absolute minimum.
- **Incredible Player Energy:** Seeing top players crush high-tier boss songs while a crowd of dozens gathered around cheering made every hour of preparation worth it.

### Reflections

Volunteering at NDK with Full Combo Arcade combined two of my biggest passions: hands-on hardware engineering and rhythm gaming culture. Working alongside such a dedicated, knowledgeable crew of arcade lovers and seeing the pure joy on attendees' faces throughout the weekend was unforgettable.

Huge thanks to the Full Combo Arcade leadership and fellow crew members for a fantastic event! Looking forward to helping out at future conventions and continuing to support the arcade community.
